# NOVA Finance: Product Overview

**Status:** Product Design Concept and Interactive Prototype (not a production banking application)
**Creative Direction:** NGOPREK DIGITAL (`https://www.ngoprekdigital.site/`)
**Source File:** `design/NOVA-Finance.figcraft` (`1,339` editable nodes across `6` pages)

## Product Vision

NOVA Finance ("Make money make sense.") explores a calmer, typography-led mobile banking experience for individuals who manage regular income, virtual card spending, and structured savings goals on their phone.

Rather than wrapping every balance, chart, and transaction inside repetitive card grids, NOVA pairs daylight editorial surfaces (`#F7F9F6` and `#FFFFFF`) with high-contrast Deep Forest (`#123C2B`) focal points and edge-to-edge ledger rows so users can scan cash flow in seconds.

## Target Audience

- Independent professionals, designers, and salaried earners tracking recurring retainers, monthly spending caps, and personal savings targets.
- Mobile-first users who rely on virtual debit cards for online subscriptions, contactless transit, and instant peer transfers.
- Users who prefer explicit status labels, clear fees, and calm visual hierarchy over gamified or cluttered fintech dashboards.
